# Global Sales Performance Dashboard

This project analyzes global retail sales data across multiple countries
using Excel and Power BI.

## Dashboard Highlights
- 💰 Total Sales: $22.35M
- 💸 Total Cost: $20.1M
- 📈 Total Profit: $2.264M
- 👥 Average Customer Age: 36

## Visuals Included
- **Sum of Profit by Product Category** – Donut chart (Accessories, Clothing, Bikes)
- **Sum of Profit by Country** – Bar chart across Germany, US, UK, France
- **Sum of Quantity by Country** – Donut chart showing quantity distribution
- **Sum of Total Sales by Month** – Line chart showing monthly sales trend

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preparation
- Power BI Desktop – Data visualization and dashboard design

## Countries Covered
- 🇩🇪 Germany
- 🇺🇸 United States
- 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
- 🇫🇷 France

## Key Insights
- Accessories is the top profitable category with 60.94%
- Germany leads in profit with $959K
- Peak sales were in May and June ($2.63M - $2.68M)
- Sales dropped significantly in summer then recovered in Q4
